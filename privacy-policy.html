Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Notes Locker ("the app", "we", "us") is a private vault app that lets you store photos, videos, and files behind a PIN, with everything encrypted on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what control you have over it.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you add to your vault — photos, videos, documents, notes — is encrypted on your device using AES-256 encryption before it is ever written to storage. Your PIN (and decoy PIN, if you set one) is never stored in plain text; only a cryptographic hash of it is kept, in your device's secure storage.

We do not have a copy of your vault contents, your PIN, or your encryption keys. We cannot access, recover, or decrypt your data. If you forget your PIN, your vault contents cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

2. Optional cloud backup

If you choose to enable cloud backup, the app builds an encrypted archive of your vault and uploads it to your own Google Drive account — not to our servers. The archive is encrypted before it leaves your device; Google Drive only ever stores the encrypted file.

The app requests access only to files it creates itself (Google's "drive.file" scope). It cannot see, list, or access any other files in your Google Drive.

Backups remain under your control: you can delete them from your Google Drive at any time, independently of the app.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium features are handled through Google Play Billing, with subscription status managed by our payments provider, RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device/app identifier and your purchase history (such as which subscription you bought and when it renews) in order to grant and verify premium access. It does not receive your vault contents, PIN, or any files you store in the app.

See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for how they handle this data: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Photos, videos, and files — to let you import items into your vault.
  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face) — as an optional, faster way to unlock the app instead of typing your PIN.
  • Internet access — only used for optional cloud backup and for verifying subscription status.

The app does not request or use camera access, microphone access, or location data for any active feature.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored vault data and encryption keys from your device. If you enabled cloud backup, the encrypted archive remains in your Google Drive until you delete it yourself — uninstalling the app does not delete your Drive backups.

7. Children's privacy

Notes Locker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
